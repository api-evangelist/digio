# Digio - Digital Trust Infrastructure

Onboard trusted users, digitally sign documents, collect recurring payments, and be DPDPA-compliant with our suite of products.

## Identity Verification Solution: KYC/KYB

- [DigiKYC Onboard users securely] (https://www.digio.in/digi-kyc/): A comprehensive solution for performing customer Know Your Customer (KYC) processes efficiently and securely. ID OCR checks, Aadhar/PAN verification, face verification, V-CIP, KRA, and CKYC. SEBI RBI-compliant onboarding flows.

## Document Management and Digital Signing

- [DigiDocs Digitize Documents & Forms] (https://www.digio.in/digi-docs/): A tool for digitizing documents and forms, streamlining the document management process.
- [DigiSign Digital Signing Platform] (https://www.digio.in/digi-sign/): A secure digital signing platform that allows users to legally e-sign documents and e-stamp paper using DSC and Aadhar e-sign, enhancing the efficiency of document workflows.

## Recurring Payment Solutions

- [DigiCollect Automate Recurring Payments] (https://www.digio.in/digi-collect/): A solution for automating recurring payment processes using NACH and UPI, simplifying billing and payment collection.

## Account Aggregation

- [DigioLink Account Aggregator] (https://www.digio.link/): An account aggregation service that consolidates financial data from multiple sources for better financial intelligence.

## AML/CFT Solution

- [DigiShield Name Screening & Continuous Transaction Monitoring] (https://www.digio.in/digi-shield/): A service that provides advanced name screening and continuous monitoring against sensitive watchlists to mitigate AML/CFT risks.

## DPDPA Data Protection and Data Privacy Solution

- [CoTrust by Digio DPDPA Privacy Solution] (https://www.digio.in/co-trust/): A privacy solution that ensures compliance with data protection regulations while managing customer data.
